College basketball ratings and forecasts

Every Division I team gets a new rating each morning, men's and women's. The same models forecast games, value players, rank pro prospects and price out the 2027 NCAA tournament. All of it runs on free public data. Each forecast uses only what was known before tip-off, and the models are graded on seasons they never saw.
