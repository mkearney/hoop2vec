Basketball ratings and forecasts

Every team in men's and women's Division I, the NBA and the WNBA gets a new rating each morning. The same models forecast games, value players and price out the 2027 NCAA tournaments and the pro playoffs. All of it runs on free public data. Each forecast uses only what was known before tip-off, and the models are graded on seasons they never saw.

How it works

Ratings

Every team is a vector of offense, defense and tempo, re-solved each morning from the games so far.

Matchup network

A neural network learns team embeddings and scores how any two teams fit together.

Team identities

Factor analysis places every team in a style space and names its archetype.

Forecasts

Each game gets a margin and a win probability, graded on seasons the model never saw.
